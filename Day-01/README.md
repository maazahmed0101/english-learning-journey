## Pronouns

> **I, You, He, She, It, We, They**

## Am / Is / Are

```text
I → am

He / She / It → is

You / We / They → are
```

> **Key rule:** `You` always uses **are**, whether it refers to one person or more.

## Sentence Structure

> **Positive:** `Subject + am/is/are + information`

> **Negative:** `Subject + am/is/are + not + information`

> **Question:** `Am/Is/Are + Subject + ... ?`

## Short Answers

> **Are you...?** → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

> **Is he...?** → Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.

> **Is she...?** → Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

> **Are they...?** → Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
